Privacy Policy — Anti Inflammatory Diet

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Anti Inflammatory Diet app ("the App") handles information when you use it. Anti Inflammatory Diet is a free app with anti-inflammatory-style recipes, aimed at a general adult audience.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we do NOT collect

The App does not require registration or a user account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal data to use the App.

The App does not collect or transmit to our servers:

  • Personally identifiable data.
  • Photos, contacts, location, messages, or files from your device.
  • Browsing history outside the App itself.

2. Information stored only on your device

For the App to work, the following data is stored locally on your phone (never sent to our servers or to third parties):

  • Favorite recipes: the IDs of the recipes you mark with the heart icon.
  • Weekly menu: the recipes you assign to each day and meal type (breakfast, lunch, dinner, dessert).

This data is automatically deleted if you uninstall the App, and you can clear it at any time by removing favorites or menu assignments, or by clearing the App's data from your phone's settings.

3. Internet connection

The App requires an internet connection to work, since it is monetized through advertising. Without a connection, the App will be unavailable until the connection is restored.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and use technical data from your device, including:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID).
  • Technical device and network information (model, operating system, approximate IP address).
  • Interactions with the ads shown (impressions, clicks).

This information is used by Google and its partners to show relevant ads and measure their performance, in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can review how Google handles this data here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy
https://www.google.com/about/company/user-consent-policy/

Consent (users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App will show you a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform – UMP) before requesting personalized ads, in compliance with the GDPR and equivalent regulations. You can change your consent choice at any time from that form when it reappears.

5. Firebase

The App does not use Firebase or any other Google infrastructure for analytics or tracking. The only third-party services integrated are Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising consent, described in Section 4.

6. App permissions

The App requests only the following permissions, strictly necessary for it to function:

PermissionWhat it's used for
Internet (INTERNET)Load AdMob ads and check connectivity.
Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)Detect whether an internet connection is available.
Advertising ID (AD_ID)Required by Google AdMob to show ads (Android 13 or higher).

The App does not request access to camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, SMS, or photos.

7. Children

The App is aimed at a general adult audience and is not designed or intentionally directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age applicable in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Informational purposes only, not medical advice

The recipes and the Body Mass Index (BMI) calculator included in the App are provided for informational purposes only and do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a healthcare professional with any questions related to your diet or health.

9. Security

Since the App does not collect personal data on its own servers (it has no user-facing servers of its own), the main data processing corresponds to the third-party services described above (Google AdMob/UMP), which apply their own security measures.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally, for example if new features or third-party services are added. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will indicate when the most recent change was made. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
